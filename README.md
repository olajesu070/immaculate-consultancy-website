## Core Services (10 Practice Areas)

1. **Company and Business Registration (CAC):**
   - Incorporation of Business Names, Private Limited Liability Companies (Ltd), Public Companies (Plc), Incorporated Trustees, NGOs, and Churches.
2. **Pre and Post Incorporation Filings:**
   - Statutory annual returns, changes in directorship and shareholders, share capital restructuring, and registered address updates.
3. **TIN Certificate (Tax Identification Number):**
   - Expedited Tax Identification Number processing via Joint Tax Board (JTB) and Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS).
4. **SCUML Certificate:**
   - Full facilitation and procurement of Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) certificates for DNFBPs and mandatory corporate banking compliance.
5. **SON Certificate:**
   - Standards Organisation of Nigeria product certification, MANCAP and SONCAP conformity documentation.
6. **Trade Mark / Patent:**
   - Comprehensive brand name and logo protection, trademark availability searches, journal publications, and patent registrations.
7. **Business / Legal Advisory:**
   - Strategic corporate counsel, commercial contract drafting and vetting, regulatory risk assessments, and dispute mitigation frameworks.
8. **Business Development:**
   - Bankable business plans, market feasibility studies, corporate pitch decks, operating models, and scalable commercial expansion frameworks.
9. **Property Management:**
   - Real estate administration, title verification (C of O, Governor's Consent, Deed of Assignment), tenancy agreements, and lease portfolio oversight.
10. **Final Year Academic Research:**
    - Rigorous research consultation: topic formulation, methodology design, literature review curation, empirical statistical analysis (SPSS, R, Stata), and dissertation support.
